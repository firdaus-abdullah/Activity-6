🎤 Debrief — Present Your Findings

Each group has 2 minutes to present their calculations and decision. Structure your presentation as follows:

  • Show which plate(s) you selected as valid — and explain why the others were excluded.
  • Walk through your CFU/g calculation — show the formula and the arithmetic.
  • State the rounded result and your acceptance decision against ≤ 1,000 CFU/g.
  • Explain what the TNTC plates at the lowest dilution tell you about the sample.
  • Justify your investigation path — which next steps did you choose and why?
